Wallet Support Policy

Customers can use their wallet for supported transactions and
merchant payments.

The wallet has a balance, currency, and status.

A wallet can have statuses such as:

ACTIVE
BLOCKED
SUSPENDED

Customers can view their current wallet balance through the
authenticated customer-support system.

The AI support agent must not expose another customer's wallet
information.

Financial transactions must use the transaction records returned by
authorized business tools. The AI agent must not directly access the
database.

For suspected unauthorized transactions, fraud, account takeover,
or other security-sensitive financial issues, the case should be
escalated to the appropriate human support team.
