Customer KYC Policy

KYC verification is required for customers before accessing certain
financial services.

Supported verification methods may include National ID and other
approved identity documents.

A KYC status can be:

VERIFIED
PENDING
REJECTED

If KYC is rejected, the customer should be informed of the rejection
reason when available and guided to complete verification again.

Customers should not share sensitive identity documents through
customer support chat unless specifically requested through an
approved secure verification flow.

KYC-related account changes and identity verification issues that
cannot be resolved through standard guidance should be escalated to
the KYC support team.
