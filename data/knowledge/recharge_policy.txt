Recharge Support Policy

Customers can perform mobile or wallet-related recharges using
supported payment methods.

Recharge statuses include:

SUCCESS
PENDING
FAILED

If a recharge is SUCCESS but the expected balance or service benefit
has not been updated, the support system should verify the recharge
transaction and current customer wallet state.

If a recharge is PENDING, the customer should be informed that the
transaction is still being processed.

If a recharge is FAILED, the customer should be informed that the
recharge did not complete successfully. The failure reason should be
provided when available.

A recharge issue that cannot be automatically resolved should be
escalated to the appropriate payments support team.
